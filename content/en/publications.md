The laboratory has published a substantial body of high-quality academic papers and patents in the field of rail transit wireless communication, covering frontier topics such as channel modeling, MIMO systems, 5G communication, and information security.

## Publication Overview

- **Journal Papers**: Over 40 papers published in prestigious international journals including IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, IEEE Communications Letters, IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, IEEE Sensors Journal, IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation Letters, IEEE Access, Sensors, Electronics, and Applied Sciences.
- **Conference Papers**: Papers on CBTC reliability analysis, ray tracing, ultra-narrowband modulation, and wireless sensor networks presented at multiple international conferences.
- **Patents**: Prof. Zheng Guoxin, as the primary inventor, has been granted over 20 invention patents covering MIMO leaky cable systems, digital filters, multipath channel simulators, ultra-narrowband communication methods, WLAN fast handover, and mobile communication simulation test platforms.

## Representative Research Topics

1. **5G high-speed rail massive MIMO channel modeling and polarization characteristics**
2. **mmWave metro tunnel beam prediction and channel measurement**
3. **LTE-M adjacent channel interference analysis and suppression**
4. **RIS (Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface) assisted tunnel communication**
5. **Leaky cable MIMO system performance and capacity analysis**
6. **Deep learning assisted field strength prediction and channel modeling**
