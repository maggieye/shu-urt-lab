The laboratory is led by **Prof. Zheng Guoxin**, supported by the academic strengths of the School of Communication and Information Engineering at Shanghai University. The team brings together researchers with deep expertise in wireless communication, channel modeling, and rail transit signaling systems.

## Academic Heritage

Since Prof. Zheng Guoxin joined Shanghai University in 2000, the laboratory has trained nearly 100 doctoral and master's students. Research directions have evolved from early UHF tunnel propagation and CBTC wireless communication systems to today's cutting-edge fields including 5G/6G massive MIMO channel modeling, mmWave communication, RIS-assisted transmission, and ISAC.

## Collaborative Network

The laboratory has established long-term collaborations with universities and enterprises worldwide:

- **Domestic Collaborations**: Casco Signal, Huawei Technologies, Shanghai Shentong Metro, Shanghai Control Safety, China Railway Communication & Signal Testing, and more
- **International Collaborations**: University of Michigan (USA), Chinese University of Hong Kong, Nanyang Technological University (Singapore)
- **Academic Organizations**: Chinese Institute of Electronics, Shanghai Electronics Society, IET (Institution of Engineering and Technology)
