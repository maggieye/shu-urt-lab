The laboratory has formed four core research directions addressing the key scientific and technical challenges of urban rail transit wireless communication. These directions complement each other, covering the full spectrum from fundamental electromagnetic theory to system engineering applications.

![Research Path](/images/research-path.png)

## Research Overview

The laboratory's research centers on **confined-space wireless communication**, encompassing multi-band channel measurement and modeling from Sub-6 GHz to mmWave, design and optimization of train control communication systems, MIMO antenna system performance analysis, and information security. Research outcomes have been successfully applied in metro communication engineering projects across Shanghai, Wuhan, Shenyang, Xi'an, Zhengzhou, Nanning, Changchun, and other cities throughout China.

## Key Techniques and Standards

The laboratory's methods and outcomes align with the following international standards:

- **3GPP TR 38.900 / TR 38.901** (0.5-100 GHz channel models)
- **ITU-R P.2040-3** (over-the-horizon propagation prediction)
- **SBR (Shooting and Bouncing Ray)** and **Wireless Insite** for ray-tracing simulation
- **BPNN / GA-BPNN** for intelligent path loss prediction
