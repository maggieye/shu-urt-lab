The Joint Laboratory of Rail Transit Wireless Communication at Shanghai University was established by **Casco Signal Ltd.**, **Shanghai China Railway Communication & Signal Testing Co., Ltd.**, and **Shanghai Control Safety Technology Co., Ltd.** Leveraging the disciplinary strengths of two doctoral programs—**Electromagnetic Fields and Microwave**, and **Communication and Information Systems**—the laboratory has long been dedicated to fundamental research and engineering applications in the field of urban rail transit wireless communication.

Focusing on the interdisciplinary challenges of **Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC)** in large-scale metro construction, the laboratory investigates electromagnetic wave propagation in tunnels and other specialized environments, the stringent requirements for low latency and high reliability in 4G/5G communications for train control, and has solved engineering challenges in confined-space wireless channel modeling and the integration of signaling and communication systems.
