## Overview

The Joint Laboratory of Rail Transit Wireless Communication at Shanghai University was established by **Casco Signal Ltd.**, **Shanghai China Railway Communication & Signal Testing Co., Ltd.**, and **Shanghai Control Safety Technology Co., Ltd.** Supported by the academic strengths of two doctoral programs at Shanghai University—**Electromagnetic Fields and Microwave** and **Communication and Information Systems**—the laboratory is committed to fundamental research and engineering applications in urban rail transit wireless communication.

The laboratory focuses on the interdisciplinary challenges of **Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC)** in large-scale metro construction, with specific research areas including:

- **Electromagnetic wave propagation theory** in tunnels and other specialized environments
- Technical requirements for **low latency and high reliability** of 4G/5G communications for train control
- Solving engineering challenges in **confined-space wireless channel modeling** and interface testing between signaling and communication systems

## Research Platforms

The laboratory has established two core research platforms:

### 1. Wireless Communication Physical Channel Platform

This platform enables systems under test to operate in physically simulated wireless transmission environments of various types, providing critical experimental conditions for channel measurement, modeling, and validation. It supports comprehensive coverage from Sub-6 GHz to mmWave bands and can simulate various typical rail transit environments including tunnels, stations, and viaducts.

### 2. Network Comprehensive Testing Platform

Used for testing reliability metrics such as **latency, throughput, and packet loss** when LTE-M carries CBTC and other services. Dedicated LTE-M information security protection equipment was deployed on this platform, and comprehensive communication performance testing was conducted after the security equipment deployment.
