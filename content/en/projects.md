## Funded Projects

Since 2009, the laboratory has undertaken numerous significant research projects, including NSFC Key and General Projects, the National 11th Five-Year S&T Support Program, Shanghai Science and Technology Commission major projects, Shanghai Zhangjiang National Innovation Demonstration Zone special projects, and corporate collaborations with Huawei Technologies.

## Awards

The laboratory's research achievements in rail transit wireless communication have received multiple prestigious science and technology awards.

## Engineering Practice

Research outcomes have been applied in metro communication engineering projects across numerous cities in China:

- **2017**: Successfully supported China's first metro line using 4G for train control—**Wuhan Line 6**—passing all tests in a single trial, marking China's formal entry into the LTE era for train control.
- **2019**: First successful deployment and testing of China's **ZUC (ZUC) cipher** algorithm for CBTC information security on **Zhengzhou Line 14** and **Shanghai Line 18**.
- **Recent years**: Supported metro communication engineering in Shanghai, Wuhan, Shenyang, Xi'an, Zhengzhou, Nanning, Changchun, and other cities.
- **2020-2023**: Collaborated with Huawei and Shanghai Shentong Metro Group on 5G metro technology research at Shanghai Metro Line 7, and 5G high-speed rail communication research at Shanghai EMU depot.

## High-Speed Testing Milestones

- Completed LTE-M communication testing at speeds of **160/180/200/250 km/h** on 3GPP-standard tracks
- End-to-end LTE-M performance validated at up to **400 km/h**, with latency ≤ 150ms
- At 540 km/h, LTE-M communication reliability reached 98% with 314 Kbit/s throughput
